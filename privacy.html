3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on the application you use, the features you access, your device settings, the permissions you grant and applicable law, we may collect the following categories of information.

3.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the Services, certain information is collected automatically, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version and build number
  • Device language and region
  • Time zone
  • Screen characteristics
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate geographic information (such as country or region) derived from your IP address
  • Technical diagnostic information
  • Usage information and interaction timestamps

This information is used for app functionality, security, performance optimisation, analytics, compatibility testing and technical support.

We do not collect precise (GPS-level) location. Where we refer to location, we mean country- or region-level information inferred from your IP address.

3.2 Usage information

We collect information about how users interact with our applications, including screens viewed, features used, navigation within the app, session duration and frequency, performance metrics, error and diagnostic events, subscription-related interactions, and general engagement and usage patterns.

This information helps us understand how our applications are used and improve their functionality, usability, reliability and performance.

3.3 Local network and connected device information

When this section applies. This section applies only where an application offers control of televisions, streaming devices, smart home devices or other compatible hardware on your local network. Applications without such features do not request local network access and do not process any of the information described below.

To provide this functionality, the application requires local network access permission on your device. It then discovers compatible devices on your local network and communicates with them directly. The following information may be processed:

  • Device type, manufacturer and model information
  • Operating system type of the connected device
  • User-assigned device names
  • Local network addresses of discovered devices
  • Connection characteristics and pairing status

Device discovery and control commands are exchanged directly between your device and the connected hardware on your local network. We do not collect the content displayed on your connected devices, your viewing history, or personal files stored on those devices. Limited technical information about connection success or failure may be included in diagnostic and crash reports so that we can improve compatibility.

You may revoke local network access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Revoking access will prevent the remote control features from working.

3.4 Purchase and subscription information

If you purchase a subscription or make an in-app purchase through the Apple App Store, Apple processes the payment transaction. We receive limited information related to your purchase, including subscription status, product identifier, transaction identifier, purchase date, renewal status and expiration information.

We do not collect or store your payment card information.

Apple acts as an independent controller in respect of the payment transaction and your App Store account relationship. Apple's handling of that information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy, not by this Policy.

3.5 Advertising identifiers and advertising data

When this section applies. This section applies only where an application displays advertising or participates in advertising attribution. See also Section 6.

Where you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, or where applicable law otherwise permits, we and our advertising partners may process:

  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or an equivalent advertising identifier
  • Ad impressions, ad clicks and ad interaction events
  • Install and re-engagement attribution data
  • Coarse location (country or region) and device characteristics used for ad delivery
  • Interest or audience segments inferred from your activity within our applications

If you decline the App Tracking Transparency request, the IDFA is not available to us and we do not use it.

3.6 Information from third-party sources

Where permitted by law, we may receive limited information from advertising and attribution partners, analytics providers, and platform providers such as Apple and Google. This information is limited to aggregated or pseudonymous campaign, attribution and performance data.

3.7 Information we do not collect

In no application, and regardless of the permissions you grant, do we collect, receive, transmit or store:

  • Your name, postal address or telephone number
  • Payment card numbers or bank details
  • Precise (GPS-level) location data
  • Your photos or videos, or their metadata — where an application provides photo management features it accesses your photo library locally on your device, as described in Section 4, but no photo, video or associated metadata ever leaves your device
  • Your contacts, calendar entries, microphone or camera content
  • The content displayed on, or files stored on, televisions and other devices you may connect to
  • Special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR (including health, biometric, genetic, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, sex life or sexual orientation data)
  • Government-issued identification numbers

4. PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS AND ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

When this section applies. This section applies only where an application offers features for reviewing, organising or deleting photos and videos stored on your device. Applications without such features never request access to your photo library, and none of the processing described below takes place in them.

Where such features are offered, the application requests access to your photo library through the standard iOS Photos permission.

All processing of your photos and videos takes place entirely on your device. This includes:

  • Detecting duplicate, similar, blurry or low-quality images
  • Identifying screenshots and other clutter
  • Grouping photos by date for month-by-month review
  • Reading location metadata embedded in photos in order to display a personal map of places
  • Image quality enhancement
  • Generating anniversary content and home screen widget content

We do not upload, transmit, receive, store or have access to your photos, videos or their metadata. We operate no server on which photos or videos are stored. No third party — including our analytics or advertising partners — receives your photos, videos or any information derived from them.

Location metadata

Photos taken on a mobile device frequently contain GPS coordinates in their EXIF metadata. Where an application displays a map of the places your photos were taken, that metadata is read and used exclusively on your device. We do not collect, receive or store precise location data.

Deletion of photos

When you choose to delete photos or videos, deletion is carried out by iOS through the system photo library. Deleted items are moved to the "Recently Deleted" album and are permanently removed by iOS according to your device settings, normally after 30 days. We are not able to recover deleted items.

Your control

You may revoke or limit photo library access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos, including by selecting "Limited Access" so that the application can see only the photos you choose. Revoking access will prevent the relevant features from working but will not affect your subscription.

Advertising and your photos

No information derived from your photos or videos is used to select, target or measure advertising. Advertising, where an application displays it, is based only on the categories of information described in Sections 3.1, 3.2 and 3.5.

6. ADVERTISING, TRACKING AND YOUR CHOICES

6.1 App Tracking Transparency

Some of our applications request permission to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency ("ATT") framework. If you grant permission, advertising identifiers and similar identifiers may be used for advertising attribution, campaign measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and improving advertising effectiveness.

If you decline permission, we do not access the IDFA and we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites. You may change your decision at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

6.2 In-app advertising

When this section applies. This section applies only where an application displays advertising. Applications that do not display advertising do not integrate advertising software development kits, and no advertising identifier is processed within them.

Where an application displays advertising, that advertising is supplied by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. Advertising may be:

  • Personalised (interest-based) — shown on the basis of your advertising identifier and inferred interests. We serve personalised advertising only where you have granted ATT permission and, in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, given consent through our in-app consent prompt.
  • Non-personalised (contextual) — shown on the basis of the content of the application and coarse information such as country. Declining tracking does not remove advertising; it makes the advertising you see less relevant.

6.3 Consent in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we present a separate consent prompt through a certified consent management platform before any personalised advertising is served or any advertising identifier is used. You may change or withdraw your choices at any time through the "Privacy choices" option within the application.

6.4 How to control advertising and tracking

  • iOS tracking permission: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking
  • Apple personalised advertising: Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising
  • In-app privacy choices: available in the settings of any application that displays advertising
  • Google advertising controls: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Meta advertising controls: https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences
  • You may also email us at deep.value4u@gmail.com and we will action your request

6.5 Analytics

We use analytics technologies to understand how users interact with our applications, evaluate feature performance, improve usability, identify technical issues and develop new functionality. Analytics data is pseudonymous and we do not attempt to re-identify you from it or combine it with directly identifying information.

The specific analytics and advertising technologies we use may change over time. Where a change materially alters the categories of data collected or the recipients of that data, we will update this Policy before the change takes effect.

11. CHILDREN

Our applications are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children.

An age rating of 4+ in the Apple App Store indicates only that an application contains no objectionable content. It does not mean that the application is designed for, targeted at, or primarily intended for children. Our applications are not published in the Apple Kids Category.

United States. Our Services are not directed to children under 13 within the meaning of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom. Where we rely on consent — including consent to tracking for advertising purposes — we do not knowingly seek that consent from a child below the age specified by national law. In Poland that age is 16 years (Article 8 GDPR). Consent given by a child below that age is not valid unless given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility.

If any of our applications is or becomes directed to children, we will, for that application:

  • not request App Tracking Transparency permission;
  • not serve personalised or interest-based advertising, and will tag all advertising requests for child-directed treatment with the relevant advertising networks;
  • not use third-party advertising or third-party analytics in any application published in the Apple Kids Category; and
  • publish an App-Specific Supplement describing the applicable practices.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child in a manner that violates applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information promptly. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at deep.value4u@gmail.com.

12. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

12.1 Rights in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and obtain a copy of it (Article 15)
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data rectified (Article 16)
  • Have your data erased in the circumstances set out in Article 17 ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict processing in the circumstances set out in Article 18
  • Receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller (Article 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to object at any time and without justification to processing for direct marketing purposes (Article 21)
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal (Article 7(3))
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects (Article 22)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Article 77). Our supervisory authority is:
